Why muscle - and why blood

Muscle doesn't fail in isolation. It takes four systems with it.

01

Cardiovascular

Low muscle mass doubles cardiovascular mortality risk. The signal is present in blood a decade before a cardiac event.

Blood reveals cardiovascular risk long before symptoms appear.
02

Metabolism & Insulin Resistance

Every 10% increase in muscle mass reduces insulin resistance by 11%. Blood tracks this shift in real time across panels.

Metabolic resilience is continuously measurable through biomarkers.
03

Bone & Joint Density

Muscle weakness is the #1 predictor of fracture risk in adults over 40. Bone decline starts at 45. Screening starts at 65.

Muscle and bone age together and blood sees both.
04

Cognitive Health

Brain function declines with muscle through inflammation, IGF-1, and insulin sensitivity — all readable in a single blood panel.

The brain and muscle are connected through shared pathways.
