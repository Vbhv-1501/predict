01Inflammation▾

Chronic low-grade inflammation is the earliest measurable signal of biological ageing. It predates every major chronic disease by years.

02Vascular Health▾

Poor circulation and vascular tone reduce oxygen delivery to muscle, accelerating decline and raising cardiovascular risk silently.

03Muscle & Bone▾

Muscle fibre loss and bone density decline are interconnected — the musculoskeletal system ages as one unit, not in isolation.

04Nerve Health▾

Peripheral nerve dysfunction reduces coordination, balance, and muscle activation — often the first sign of diabetic or metabolic nerve damage.

05Renal Function▾

Kidney filtration capacity directly reflects systemic metabolic load. eGFR decline is both a consequence and an early warning of muscle-linked disease.

06Metabolic Health▾

Insulin resistance, HbA1c trajectory, and lipid dysregulation are downstream consequences of muscle losing its role as the body's primary glucose sink.
